Dataset¶

This dataset contains historical loan data for 346 customers, capturing whether their loans were successfully paid off or ended up in default (collection). The dataset includes the following key features:

Loan_status: Indicates the loan outcome—Paid Off (repaid successfully) or Collection (defaulted).

Principal: The original loan amount borrowed by the customer.

Terms: The repayment schedule, which can be weekly (7 days), biweekly, or monthly.

Effective_date: The date when the loan was approved and became active.

Due_date: The final repayment due date (since this is a one-time payoff loan structure).

Age: The age of the applicant at the time of borrowing.

Education: The education level of the borrower.

Gender: The gender of the applicant (likely categories: Male, Female, or Other).

Conclusion¶

For both genders:Loans with lower principal amounts are more frequently paid off.As principal amount increases, the proportion of loans going into collection tends to increase.For Males:A broader spread of loan amounts is observed.More unpaid loans (collection status) appear in the higher principal ranges.For Females:Loan amounts might be slightly lower on average.Fewer instances of loans in collection, suggesting better repayment behavior in this sample.Loan repayment seems negatively associated with higher principal amounts.Gender differences in loan behavior may exist but should be confirmed with statistical testing.

    knn.fit(train_X, train_y)
    pred_i = knn.predict(test_X)
    error_rate_test.append(np.mean(pred_i != test_y))



error_rate_train = []
for y in range(1,KK):
    
    knn = KNeighborsClassifier(n_neighbors=y)
    knn.fit(train_X, train_y)
    pred_i = knn.predict(train_X)
    #print(pred_i != train_y)
    error_rate_train.append(np.mean(pred_i != train_y))
    plt.figure(figsize=(10,6))
plt.plot(range(1,KK),error_rate_test, linestyle='-', marker='o',label="test",

plt.plot(range(1,KK),error_rate_train, linestyle='-', marker='o',label="train",
